### *Interactive Fiction*

This is the interactive fiction interface of the angelic fantasy narrative—Angelic Earthling. It is very simple; we begin with the same introduction that the main work introduces and get prompted with binary yes and no; do it and don’t it questions that diverge and compound into alternative storylines to the canonical one present in the working book.

The behind-the-scene code uses coordinates to locate where in the story one is at any given time; coordinates given by you—the user’s—answers to the questions incrementally. Therefore, one can use these binary coordinates to artificially reach any pathway they have a particular interest in…such as the canonical timeline.

That canonical timeline is going to be ahead of the rest of the divergents so that it may keep up with the canonical writing; the program will cease once the end of a branch is reached but this branch will extend far beyond so if you want to just use this to get a very primitive rundown of the (to date) canon of Angelic Earthling...

Run 101101

That is, answer “yes : 1” then “ no : 0” then “yes : 1” and so on.



###### Beyond just the administrative information, this is the universe itself:



# *Angelic Earthling*

A story that is both mythological and literal; that is, the narrative that beats the heart of the people of Pacifia and spirits their power against the warring factions of the world in angelic form.



## *Saga of Pacifia*

Twin angels Rosus and Rosibus are exiled from the unseen world into the seen world where their forceful entry into the earth wrings substantive change with them which causes a disagreement so fierce that they kill each other. They also wrought a substantive change onto the people of Pacifia forcing them to take up a new vocational tradition; one which is too high-octane for longstanding survival. In response, Rotimi and Rumuala swear and endeavour to change the tradition by changing their people for the better.



## *Saga of Atlantia*

The Aquatic Nomu produced by the Atlantic people cause havoc in many lands including Pacifia. Rotimi and Rumuala face another temporary destruction in their own destroying of the mindless creatures of water flesh. During which there are many power struggles for the remnant of the Nomu and country of the Pacifics from which they have been displaced. After which Rigata and Rumiri seek vengeance against the nation by sinking their combat resources…and the rest of them.



## *Saga of Indiana*

The Pacifics, other peoples and some of the Nomu were commissioned as low-caste labourers by the Indianics to rebuild their infrastructures of beauty of power—the animate Statues and the Amusement Park—which were damaged by the insurgence of the Nomu. However complex and fortified the Park is and how strong and recurring the remaining Nomu and the Statues are, Ralum and Rikpughi will not backdown from either levelling it or taking it over—whichever one best saves their nation.

## *Saga of North and South*

The Pacific, Atlantic and Indianic epics make the founding mythology of the Idemilians devised long ago to be an attempted defence against conquering peoples whose gods and mythologies remained stronger than their own, leaving the people to be scattered in strange lands. Idemilians Rosé of the south and Rougé in the north are graced to participate in other outcast projects in which the mythos and spirit of the people shall consolidate into a free nation.

## *Saga of Castle*

The entire world had been primed into a painful absolutism because of how great the Olympians were. There were futile attempts to image them. The Idemilians were one of these futilities; holding on to castles not local to them and, therefore, doing far too much damage than enriching. Therefore, they must clear this soft oppression so they can be liberated to achieve their full potential.

## *Saga of Bastions*

Empowered greatly with a great bastion of gods to strike and destroy other bastions of gods, the Idemilians draw an indomitable degree of power out of one another through rivalry and bring it together to dominate the world around them. Provoked by nothing but the will to power and glory.

## *Saga of Curses*

The Idemilians soar high with their barbaric spirit which is soon under threat to the paralysis of slave morality: the condemnation of the dark glories of determinism, inequality, hierarchy, segragation and death. Such would only threaten the Idemilians because a lot of them are beginning to fall for this spell allowing their mythology and bastion of gods to be cracked by this new bastion of winglessness.

## *Saga of Winging*

Slave morality let the world dive into chaos by weakening first the Idemilians, letting them become ravaged by foreigners and ruled by weak tyrants, while the castles of the world are brought forth once again which continue to be a soft oppression in place of the liberating barbaric spirit. The only hope is the restoration of that winged bastion...*of angels*.
